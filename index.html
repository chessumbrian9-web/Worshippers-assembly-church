About Exploits Worship City

Exploits Worship City is a Christ-centered church in Kitale, Trans Nzoia County, Kenya. We are committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, raising disciples, restoring lives through prayer, worship, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

🙏 Our Vision

To raise Kingdom champions who impact the world through the power of God.

📖 Our Mission

Preaching the Gospel, transforming lives, and making disciples of all nations.

❤️ Our Values

Love, Faith, Prayer, Worship, Integrity, Excellence, and Service.
