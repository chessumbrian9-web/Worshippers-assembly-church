About Exploits Worship City

Exploits Worship City is a Christ-centered church committed to raising disciples through the Word of God, passionate worship, prayer, and community outreach. We believe every believer is called to fulfill God's purpose and impact the world for Christ.

Our Vision

To raise kingdom ambassadors who transform lives through the power of the Gospel.

Our Mission

To preach Jesus Christ, equip believers, disciple nations, and demonstrate God's love through service.

Core Values

Prayer • Worship • Faith • Love • Excellence • Integrity • Evangelism
